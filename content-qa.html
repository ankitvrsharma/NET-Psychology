Expert-Likeness Audit Center

Use this workspace to check whether every piece of study content is accurate, useful, exam-relevant, and genuinely helpful to a NET Psychology learner. The audit checks questions, micro-topic explanations, Quick Learn Cards, Deep Dive explanations, and Active Recall exercises before learners use them.

What happens when content needs improvement: REVIEW or ISSUE items are held back, rewritten, and audited again. This continues until the content passes or reaches the maximum of two automatic rewrite cycles. Items that still do not pass are held for your manual owner decision. The score measures content quality; it does not claim or infer human authorship.
